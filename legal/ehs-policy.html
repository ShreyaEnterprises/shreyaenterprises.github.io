EHS Policy

No Safety, Know Pain. Know Safety, No Pain.

Introduction

Management is firmly committed to a policy enabling all work activities to be carried out safely, and with all possible measures taken to approach zero risk to the health, safety and hygiene of workers and others stakeholders who may be affected by our operations.

Application of This Policy

We seek the co-operation of our workers, customers and other stakeholders to adhere safety policy and systems. Our team have strong commitment for realising safety objectives to create a safe working environment with a zero rate of accident.

Quality Policy

M/s SHREYA ENTERPRISES are committed to fulfil customer’s quality requirement and project specification to achieve customer’s satisfaction. By following best quality management principals. Our Constant Endeavour to provide efficient leadership, creativity in work and training to keep pace with latest Technological development to meet and support customer expectation, growth of organization and employees. We maintain highest standards of SAFETY, HEALTH, & ENVIRONMENT at the work place. We will try achieving best position as a leader in Engineering & construction Field.

Responsibilities

Will provide and maintain:

  • Safe working environment.
  • Safe systems of procedure
  • Keeping plant and surrounding in safe condition.
  • Providing good facilities for the welfare of workers.
  • Sharing / Training necessary information, instruction, and supervision that is necessary to ensure safe working and avoid risks to health and injury.
  • Commitment to continuously improve our performance through effective safety management.
