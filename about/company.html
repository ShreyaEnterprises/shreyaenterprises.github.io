WELCOME TO

SHREYA ENTERPRISES

The company has been working in the fields of construction and engineering for more than a decade with Partner Company M/s Ambica Enterprises.

M/s Shreya Enterprises was founded by Mr. Rajeev Kumar Tiwari during the year 2016. The company has grown by its strength, mainly manpower and its rich experience. We are successful in earning and maintaining goodwill in all our areas of operation on account of our sincerity in dealings, quality consciousness of our men and our enthusiasm to deliver goods & services in time.

M/s Shreya Enterprises' success over the years is due to its employees' dedication to excellent work, customer service that exceeds expectations, and a safety program that has led to millions of zero accident hours. Honesty and open communication have empowered M/s Shreya Enterprises workforce since day one, and it shows in our work.
